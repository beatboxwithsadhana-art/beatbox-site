Why Beatbox

Beatboxing, for me, is a form of expression, and I believe everyone has it in them. If you can hum, you are already beatboxing. I like to merge beatboxing with anything; even the slightest tempo will do. That's the magic of beatboxing—it can convert anything into music. You don't need any instruments; you are enough.

This curiosity led me to discover new sounds in beatboxing and become the first female beatboxer in Mumbai. I enjoy performing live, engaging my audience, and making them move to my beats.

About me

Active since 2017 as Mumbai’s first female beatboxer, Sadhana Tawde is a decorated vocal percussionist, host, and educator. From appearing in Mohit Suri's Saiyaara and national Myntra ads to jamming with Tom Thum and winning Female Colossal Vice Champion (2019), her work spans mainstream cinema, classical jugalbandis, and high-energy live showcases. Beyond the stage, Sadhana anchored the Street Hip-Hop Vodcast, co-organized Mumbai's first forest music festival (Aarey Music Festival), and has been mentoring learners of all ages through 1-on-1 coaching and regional community workshops.
